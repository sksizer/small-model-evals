Lemon Ginger Loaf

Serves 8 · Prep 15 min · Bake 50 min at 175 °C

Ingredients

  • 225 g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 170 g caster sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • Zest of 2 lemons
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 120 ml milk

Method

  1. Line a 900 g loaf tin.
  2. Whisk the dry ingredients, then beat in the rest.
  3. Bake until a skewer comes out clean.
