# Garden co-op planning meeting, October

Attendees: Ana (chair), Ben, Chidi, Dana, Eli, Farah. Notes taken by Ben.

## Budget

The co-op closed the season with a surplus of 412 dollars after paying the water bill, the seed order and the insurance renewal. Ana proposed keeping 250 dollars as a reserve for next spring's water bill, which last year arrived two weeks before the first plot fees were collected. Chidi asked whether the reserve could earn interest; nobody knew of an account that would pay anything meaningful on that amount, so the group agreed to keep it in the existing account. The remaining 162 dollars will go toward replacing the two broken hose reels by the east beds and buying a second wheelbarrow. Dana will collect quotes for both and report back by the November meeting.

## Plots and the waiting list

Eleven plots are active and four people are on the waiting list. Eli reported that plot 7 has not been worked since July and the holder has not answered two messages. The rules say a plot left unworked for eight weeks can be reassigned after one written notice. Farah will send the written notice this week. If there is no reply in fourteen days, plot 7 goes to the first person on the waiting list, who has already said they would take it for the winter cover crop.

There was a longer discussion about splitting the two largest plots in half so that more people on the waiting list can join. Ben was in favour because the large plots are hard for one household to keep up. Chidi was against, because the current holders were promised full plots when they joined and splitting them now feels like changing the deal. The group agreed not to split existing plots but to offer half plots to anyone who joins from now on, and to ask the current holders of the large plots whether they would volunteer to share.

## Compost

The compost bays are working well, but the third bay is full of woody material that is not breaking down. Dana suggested renting a chipper for one weekend in November. Rental is about 90 dollars a day. Eli knows a neighbour who owns one and may lend it in exchange for a share of the finished compost. Eli will ask before anyone books a rental.

People are still putting cooked food and bread in the bins, which attracts rats. Ana will print a new sign listing what can and cannot go in, with pictures, and put it on the gate and on each bay. Farah suggested a short demonstration at the spring open day; the group liked the idea and parked it until the spring planning meeting.

## Water

The east tap leaks at the joint. Ben tried tightening it and it got worse. A plumber quoted 140 dollars to replace the fitting and the first metre of pipe. Since this is a safety and waste issue, the group agreed to pay for it out of the reserve and top the reserve back up from spring plot fees. Ben will book the plumber for a weekday morning so that someone can let them in.

Chidi raised the idea of a rain barrel at each end of the site. Two barrels with diverters cost about 180 dollars in total. This was put on the wish list for next year's budget rather than this year's surplus.

## Events

The harvest share is set for the second Saturday in November, from ten until one. Everyone brings what they have left and takes what they can use; the rest goes to the food bank on Elm Street. Farah will confirm with the food bank that they can take fresh produce on a Saturday afternoon. Dana will bring the folding tables. Ana will post the date on the noticeboard and in the group chat.

## Actions

Dana: hose reel and wheelbarrow quotes, folding tables for the harvest share. Farah: written notice for plot 7, confirm with the food bank. Eli: ask the neighbour about the chipper. Ana: compost sign, post the harvest share date. Ben: book the plumber.

Next meeting: the first Tuesday in November, seven in the evening, in the shed if it is dry and at Ana's house if it rains.
