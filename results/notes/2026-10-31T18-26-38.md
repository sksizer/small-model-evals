# Run 2026-10-31T18-26-38: notes

The first run, on an Apple M4 Mac mini with 16 GiB. Its records are in `results/ledger/`; these are
the observations the numbers alone do not carry. This repository carries its classify, extract and
perf records; its screenshot-reading and orchestrate records used private material and stay out.

- Every perf row comes from a fresh load. The first pass measured each model after its quality
  suites had run, and those numbers drifted: `qwen3:4b` and `qwen3.5:4b` slowed from ~30 to ~11
  tok/s, `qwen3.5:4b` reported 9.8 GB and `qwen3.5:9b` 13.5 GB loaded. Fresh loads gave 4.2 and 7.8
  GB at steady speed. Cause not found; `scripts/run.ts` should unload between suites, not only
  between models.
- Ollama 0.40.11 ignores `num_ctx` for the qwen3.5 models: /api/ps reports 4096 whatever is asked.
  Their 8K and 32K rows are the 4K load again ("cached").
- `gemma4:e4b` reports 0.81 GB loaded at 4K for a 9.6 GB file. /api/ps likely leaves out its
  per-layer embeddings; treat its memory column as unreliable.
- `qwen3:4b`'s orchestrate latency (10.3 s median) is several times the others'; check whether it
  honours `think: false` with tools.
- Quality cases are 15–20 per suite: one case is 5–7 points. Differences of one or two cases are
  noise.

## Snapshots

This run predates suite snapshots and case ids. Its records name fingerprints from the first
scheme; `results/suites/<suite>/<old fp>.json` holds each suite as it stood when the records were
saved, marked `legacy` with the fingerprint the content computes to now. Case ids and the one-file
layout were added afterwards without changing any case's content, and the perf records come from
the fresh-load reruns, which used the current perf config.
